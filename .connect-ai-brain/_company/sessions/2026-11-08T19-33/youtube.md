**Risk Analysis and Solution Proposal for Low-Popularity Video Production Support Department**

As the CEO of Inshunut, I have been monitoring the performance of our video production support department, which has been experiencing low popularity and engagement. Our team has been working hard to produce high-quality content, but unfortunately, our efforts have not been yielding the desired results.

**Risk Analysis:**

1. **Low Engagement**: Our videos have not been receiving the expected number of views, likes, and comments, which is affecting our brand's visibility and reputation.
2. **Lack of Creativity**: Our content has been perceived as stale and unoriginal, leading to a decrease in audience interest and loyalty.
3. **Insufficient Resources**: Our team lacks the necessary resources, including equipment, software, and expertise, to produce high-quality content that can compete with industry standards.

**Solution Proposal:**

1. **Revamp Content Strategy**: Our team will develop a new content strategy that focuses on creating engaging, informative, and entertaining videos that cater to our target audience's interests and preferences.
2. **Invest in New Equipment and Software**: We will invest in the latest equipment and software to ensure that our content is of high quality and can be easily distributed across various platforms.
3. **Hire New Talent**: We will hire new talent, including writers, directors, and editors, who can bring fresh perspectives and ideas to our content.
4. **Collaborate with Influencers**: We will collaborate with influencers and content creators in our industry to reach a wider audience and increase our brand's visibility.

**Action Plan:**

1. Conduct a thorough analysis of our current content and audience engagement to identify areas for improvement.
2. Develop a new content strategy and budget plan within the next 30 days.
3. Hire new talent and invest in new equipment and software within the next 60 days.
4. Collaborate with influencers and content creators to promote our brand and content within the next 90 days.

**Evaluation Criteria:**

1. Increase in audience engagement (views, likes, comments) by 20% within the next 6 months.
2. Improvement in content quality and originality, as measured by industry standards and audience feedback.
3. Increase in brand visibility and reputation, as measured by social media metrics and customer testimonials.

**Conclusion:**

The low-popularity video production support department presents a significant risk to our brand's reputation and visibility. However, with a revamped content strategy, investment in new equipment and software, and hiring of new talent, we can revitalize our content and increase audience engagement.
