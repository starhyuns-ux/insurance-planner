1. A written report detailing our channel's performance and monetization opportunities (approx. 2-3 pages).
2. Visualizations (e.g., charts, graphs) illustrating the key findings and trends.
3. Recommendations for optimizing our content and increasing engagement.

**Deadline:** 24 hours from receipt of this directive.

**Priority:** High

**Assign to:** [Leo] (Head of YouTube)
