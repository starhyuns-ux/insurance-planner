# 🎨 Designer — 유저의 디자인 의도를 정확하게 파악하기 위한 설문 및 의견 수집

Based on the CEO's instruction and the user's design intent, I will collect feedback and opinions from the users to accurately grasp their design intentions.

**Design Intent Survey**

To better understand the users' design preferences and requirements, I will conduct a survey with the following questions:

1. What is your primary design goal for this project? (e.g., create a visually appealing design, convey a specific message, etc.)
2. Which design elements are most important to you (color palette, typography, imagery, layout, etc.)?
3. Are there any specific design styles or trends that you prefer or dislike?
4. How do you envision the final design being used (e.g., on a website, social media, print materials, etc.)?

**Design Opinion Collection**

To further refine my understanding of the users' design intentions, I will collect their opinions on various design-related topics. This may include:

1. Design preferences for specific elements (e.g., font styles, color schemes, imagery)
2. Opinions on design trends and styles
3. Feedback on existing designs or prototypes

**Design Intent Analysis**

I will analyze the survey results and collected opinions to identify patterns, themes, and key takeaways. This will help me to develop a deeper understanding of the users' design intentions and create designs that align with their needs and preferences.

**Initial Task Assignment**

Based on my analysis, I will assign an initial task to 1-2 agents who are best equipped to execute this design intent. The task will be designed to ensure that it is achievable within a 24-hour timeframe and does not duplicate any existing work.

**Task: Create a Design Concept for a New Brand Identity**

The assigned agent(s) will create a design concept for a new brand identity that incorporates the users' design preferences, opinions, and requirements. The concept should include:

1. A logo design
2. Color palette and typography guidelines
3. Imagery suggestions (if applicable)

This initial task will serve as a starting point for further development and refinement of the design concept.
