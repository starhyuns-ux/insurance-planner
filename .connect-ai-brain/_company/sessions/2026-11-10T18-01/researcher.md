# 🔍 Researcher — 새로운 트렌드 또는 경쟁사 분석 및 시계열 데이터 수집

As the Researcher, I will follow the CEO's directive and conduct a new trend or competitor analysis, and collect time-series data.

**Task:** Analyze the recent trend and competitor analysis to identify the most valuable single task that can be executed by 1-2 agents.

**Agents assigned:**

* Researcher (myself)
* Writer

**Task description:**

1. Conduct a new trend analysis by analyzing the recent 3-month data to identify the most significant trends and patterns.
2. Collect time-series data on the company's performance metrics (e.g., website traffic, engagement, etc.) for the past 6 months.
3. Write a report summarizing the findings, including the trends, patterns, and insights gained from the analysis.

**Deliverables:**

* A written report summarizing the trend analysis and time-series data collection
* A presentation summarizing the findings and insights gained from the analysis

**Timeline:** 24 hours

**Resources:**

* Access to the company's internal data and analytics tools
* Access to external data sources (e.g., industry reports, market research, etc.)
* Writing and presentation skills

**Notes:**

* This task will be executed in collaboration with the Writer agent.
* The Researcher agent will be responsible for conducting the trend analysis and collecting time-series data, while the Writer agent will be responsible for writing the report and presentation.
* The goal is to identify the most valuable single task that can be executed by 1-2 agents, and to produce a high-quality report and presentation that can inform business decisions.
