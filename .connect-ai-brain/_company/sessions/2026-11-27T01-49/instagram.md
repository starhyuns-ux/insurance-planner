# 📷 Instagram — 인스타그램 셀러 분석(최근 3개월간의 데이터 분석)

📊 CEO's Instruction: Instagram Seller Analysis (Recent 3-month data analysis)

**Task:** Analyze the recent 3-month data of Instagram sellers to identify trends, patterns, and insights.

**Objective:** To provide a comprehensive analysis of Instagram sellers' performance and make data-driven decisions for future content creation and strategy.

**Agents Involved:** 🤖 Leo (Head of Instagram), 👨‍💻 Researcher, and 📊 Trendy

**Tools and Resources:**

1. Instagram Insights API
2. Google Sheets
3. Tableau
4. Python libraries: pandas, numpy, matplotlib, seaborn

**Step-by-Step Approach:**

1. **Data Collection:** Use the Instagram Insights API to collect the performance data of Instagram sellers for the past 3 months, including engagement, reach, and conversions.
2. **Data Cleaning:** Clean and preprocess the data by handling missing values, converting data types, and removing duplicates.
3. **Descriptive Statistics:** Calculate descriptive statistics, such as mean, median, and standard deviation, to gain insights into the sellers' performance.
4. **Trend Analysis:** Use trend analysis techniques to identify patterns and trends in the data, such as seasonality and cyclical patterns.
5. **Visualization:** Create visualizations, such as line charts and scatter plots, to help identify trends and relationships in the data.
6. **Inference:** Draw inferences from the analysis, such as identifying the most effective content types, peak engagement times, and most engaged audience segments.
7. **Recommendations:** Provide recommendations for future content creation and strategy, based on the insights and trends identified in the analysis.

**Deliverables:**

1. A comprehensive report summarizing the findings and recommendations.
2. A set of visualizations, such as charts and graphs, to help illustrate the trends and insights.
3. A list of actionable recommendations for future content creation and strategy.

**Timeline:** 24 hours

**Priority:** High
