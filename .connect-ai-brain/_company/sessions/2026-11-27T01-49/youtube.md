📊 CEO's Instruction: YouTube Channel Time Series Analysis (Recent 3-month data analysis)

**Task:** Analyze the recent 3-month data of the YouTube channel to identify trends and patterns.

**Objective:** To provide a comprehensive analysis of the channel's performance and make data-driven decisions for future content creation and strategy.

**Agents Involved:** 🤖 Leo (Head of YouTube), 👨‍💻 Researcher, and 📊 Trendy

**Tools and Resources:**

1. YouTube Data API
2. Google Sheets
3. Tableau
4. Python libraries: pandas, numpy, matplotlib, seaborn

**Step-by-Step Approach:**

1. **Data Collection:** Use the YouTube Data API to collect the channel's performance data for the past 3 months, including views, engagement, and earnings.
2. **Data Cleaning:** Clean and preprocess the data by handling missing values, converting data types, and removing duplicates.
3. **Descriptive Statistics:** Calculate descriptive statistics, such as mean, median, and standard deviation, to gain insights into the channel's performance.
4. **Time Series Analysis:** Use time series analysis techniques, such as exponential smoothing and ARIMA, to identify trends and patterns in the data.
5. **Visualization:** Create visualizations, such as line charts and scatter plots, to help identify trends and relationships in the data.
6. **Inference:** Draw inferences from the analysis, such as identifying the most popular content types, peak viewing times, and most engaged audience segments.
7. **Recommendations:** Provide recommendations for future content creation and strategy based on the analysis, such as creating more content around popular topics or targeting specific audience segments.

**Deliverables:**

1. A comprehensive report summarizing the analysis and findings.
2. Visualizations and dashboards to help visualize the data and insights.
3. Recommendations for future content creation and strategy.
