**Researcher's Task**

Goal: Analyze the change in YouTube channel viewing behavior over the past 3 months and collect time-series data.

**Task Breakdown**

1. **Data Collection**: Collect time-series data on YouTube channel views, likes, comments, and engagement metrics for the past 3 months.
2. **Behavioral Analysis**: Analyze the changes in viewer behavior, including:
	* Time of day/day of the week when viewers are most active
	* Top-performing content types (e.g., videos, livestreams)
	* Most engaged audience segments (e.g., demographics, interests)
3. **Pattern Identification**: Identify patterns and trends in the data, such as:
	* Seasonal fluctuations in viewer engagement
	* Correlations between different metrics (e.g., views and likes)
4. **Insights Generation**: Generate insights from the analysis, including:
	* Suggestions for optimizing content creation and publishing schedules
	* Recommendations for improving engagement and conversion rates

* Researcher (Trend & Data Researcher) - responsible for collecting and analyzing the data
