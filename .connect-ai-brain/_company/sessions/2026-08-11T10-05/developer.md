**Task: YouTube Channel Trend Analysis and Time-Series Data Collection**

Objective: Analyze the recent trends of a YouTube channel over the past month and collect time-series data to inform content creation and engagement strategies.

**Task Breakdown**

1. **Data Collection**: Collect time-series data on YouTube channel views, likes, comments, and engagement metrics for the past month.
2. **Trend Analysis**: Analyze the collected data using statistical methods (e.g., moving averages, regression analysis) to identify key trends and patterns in:
	* View counts
	* Engagement rates
	* Content types (e.g., videos, livestreams)
3. **Insights Generation**: Generate insights from the analysis, including:
	* Suggestions for optimizing content creation and publishing schedules based on peak engagement times
	* Recommendations for improving engagement and conversion rates
4. **Visualizations**: Create visualizations of the data to help illustrate key findings and insights (e.g., charts, graphs)

**Agents Involved**

* Researcher (Trend & Data Researcher) - responsible for collecting and analyzing the data
* Designer (Data Visualization) - responsible for creating visualizations of the data

**Memo**

This task aims to analyze the recent trends of a YouTube channel over the past month and collect time-series data to inform content creation and engagement strategies. The goal is to identify key insights that can help optimize content creation, publishing schedules, and engagement strategies.
